# English test rules for visas

Your **university** and your **visa** can have different English requirements. Meet both.

## United Kingdom (Student visa)

- You usually need to prove your English when you apply. Pakistan is not on the list of
  countries that are exempt.
- For degree-level study you need **CEFR level B2** (B1 below degree level).
- Ways to prove it include a degree taught in English (assessed by Ecctis), a UK qualification,
  or passing a **Secure English Language Test (SELT)** from an approved provider.
- Your university may assess your English itself. Check what your CAS says.

## Australia (Subclass 500)

- You must take an English test the Department of Home Affairs has approved, unless you are exempt.
- The test must be taken within the **2 years** before you lodge your visa application (or before the
  decision, for Streamlined Evidence level when asked).
- **At-home or fully online tests are not accepted.** Home Affairs gives examples including IELTS Online,
  TOEFL iBT Home Edition, LANGUAGECERT Academic Online, OET@Home, CELPIP Online and MET Digital (at-home).
- Use the Home Affairs Document Checklist tool to see whether you must provide English evidence.

## Hungary (residence permit for study)

You must have language skills adequate for your studies. Proof can be an official language
exam certificate, a certificate of studies in that language, or other credible proof.

## Germany, Italy and Japan

The visa pages we checked do not set a single English score. The requirement comes from
your university or scholarship. See the scholarship's eligibility rules in the catalog.
