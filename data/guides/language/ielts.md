# IELTS in Pakistan

IELTS is the English test most scholarships in our catalog accept. In Pakistan it is run by
IDP and the British Council.

## Test format

- According to IDP Pakistan, IELTS (Academic and General Training) is now taken **on computer**.
  Listening, Reading and Writing are on a computer, and Speaking is face to face with an examiner.
- Most scholarships and universities ask for **IELTS Academic**.

## Booking

- Book online through IDP's booking system (https://bxsearch.ielts.idp.com/wizard): choose a test venue and session.
- Upload a clear copy of a valid ID, such as your passport.
- Candidates under 18 need written parental consent.
- **Fees** are shown when you book. Check the current fee there; this guide does not list it because it changes.
- IDP lists test cities including Abbottabad, Bahawalpur, Faisalabad, Gujranwala, Gujrat,
  Hyderabad, Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore, Mardan, Mirpur, Multan, Quetta, Rawalpindi,
  Sahiwal, Sargodha and Sialkot.

## Cancelling or postponing

If you cancel or postpone **more than 5 weeks** before the test, you get a refund minus an
administration fee. Within 5 weeks, the full fee is charged unless you have a serious
medical reason.

## Results and validity

- Results usually come in **about 2 days**.
- Scores are band scores from **1 to 9**.
- The Test Report Form is valid for **two years**. Each organisation can set its own validity
  period, so check with your university or visa office.

## Which test is accepted where

See the guide "English test rules for visas" for UK and Australia visa rules, including the
fact that Australia does **not** accept at-home online tests such as IELTS Online.
