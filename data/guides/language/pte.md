# PTE Academic

PTE Academic is Pearson's English test. Several scholarships in our catalog accept it,
for example Australia Awards and Destination Australia.

## Format and booking

- One computer-based test, taken at a Pearson test centre.
- You can book as little as 24 hours in advance: https://mypte.pearsonpte.com/test/search?examCode=PTE-A
- The fee and nearest centre are shown at https://www.pearsonpte.com/test-centers-and-fees/ . Check the current fee there.

## Scores and results

- The overall score is from **10 to 90**.
- Results usually come in about **2 business days** (Pearson says "results in 48 hours").
- The score report is valid for **two years** from the test date. Some institutions set a
  different period, so check with yours.
