# HEC degree attestation

The Higher Education Commission (HEC) attests university degrees, transcripts, provisional
certificates and HEC equivalence letters. Most scholarships and visas ask for HEC-attested
documents.

## The new online system

- Attestation is now **fully online and paperless**. You do not visit HEC or send original documents.
- Apply any time (24/7) at **https://eservices.hec.gov.pk**.
- After approval you receive an **e-Attestation Certificate**, which you download from your HEC account.
- Attestation of photocopies is no longer offered.

## Fee

**Rs 3,000 per document** (degree, transcript, provisional certificate or HEC equivalence
letter). The fee is not refunded, adjusted or transferred if you select the wrong documents,
so choose carefully.

Payment is through 1-Link (1-Bill): click "Proceed for Payment" to get a Consumer Number, pay
through online banking, then click "Verify Payment". The application is submitted only after
the payment is verified.

## Steps

1. **Sign up / sign in** at eservices.hec.gov.pk and choose "Degree Attestation".
2. **Personal details.** Your name, father's name and date of birth must match your CNIC and all
   documents exactly, otherwise attestation will not start.
3. **Degree details.** Level, duration, degree title, campus/college, session and registration
   or roll number. If your degree appears under several titles (e.g. "MBA" and "Master of
   Business Administration"), check each one for your college.
4. **Attestation details.** Select only the documents you need.
5. **Upload** clear PDF scans of your CNIC (front and back) and documents, plus a passport-size
   photo with a plain blue or white background.
6. **Verify** everything and accept the undertaking.
7. **Pay** (see Fee).

## What happens next

- HEC checks your application (details verified with NADRA, documents complete and consistent).
- If something is missing, it comes back under "Task Assigned to Me" / "Revision Requested".
  Fix it and resubmit.
- Your university verifies the documents against its records.
- HEC issues the e-Attestation Certificate. You are told by SMS and email.

## Rules to know

- Documents with overwriting, erasures or alterations are not attested. Get a corrected copy from your university.
- Transcripts can be attested before the final degree is issued. Incomplete transcripts must show the status clearly.
- **Foreign degrees are not attested by HEC.** HEC issues an equivalence letter for foreign qualifications, and only that letter is attested.
- Medical/nursing degrees: upload your PMDC/PNMC registration under "Other documents".
- MS/MPhil programs launched after 7 November 2013 need an HEC-issued NOC.
