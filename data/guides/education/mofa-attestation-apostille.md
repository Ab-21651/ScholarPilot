# MOFA attestation and apostille

After HEC or IBCC, many countries also want your documents attested or **apostilled** by the
Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA).

## Which comes first

MOFA's rules for educational documents:
- School certificates from Boards (BISE) must first be countersigned by **IBCC**.
- University degrees must first be attested by **HEC**.
- Technical certificates go through the local technical board and NAVTTC.
- Foreign educational documents: their IBCC or HEC equivalence is what gets attested.

So the order is: **Board/University verification → IBCC or HEC → MOFA (attestation or apostille)**.

## Apostille or attestation?

- Pakistan joined the Hague **Apostille Convention**, and it entered into force for Pakistan on
  **9 March 2023** (HCCH status table). Pakistan declared that it does not apply the Convention to India.
- An apostille is a single certificate that replaces embassy legalisation in countries that are
  also party to the Convention. Check what your university or visa office asks for. Italian
  regional grant offices, for example, accept either legalisation or an apostille.

## New digital system (from 12 October 2026)

- MOFA and its liaison offices (Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar, Quetta, Gujrat) now use a **Digital
  Attestation System** for General and Apostille attestation.
- You submit documents online and receive an **electronic certificate (e-Certificate) by email**
  after paying the fee. In most cases you do not need to visit in person.
- Portal: **https://portal-attestation.mofa.gov.pk**

## Apostille steps

From 27 July 2026, apostille requests are not accepted without online registration.
1. Create an account at https://apostille.mofa.gov.pk/
2. Fill in your details and attach both sides of your CNIC.
3. Create a new application and attach the documents to be apostilled.
4. Review and submit.
5. Wait for approval by email, then receive a challan by email.
6. Pay the challan online, through EasyPaisa or a bank.
7. Visit the selected office on the given date, only after your approval email.

Help desk (WhatsApp): +92 307 1339682.

## Apostille fees (revised 8 July 2024)

- Personal and educational documents: **Rs 3,000 per document**
- Legal documents: Rs 4,500 per document
- Commercial documents: Rs 12,000 per document

Authorised courier companies (Gerry's, Leopards, TCS) charge their own service fee on top.

## General attestation

- MOFA's older instructions say no fee is charged for general attestation (only revenue stamps),
  but the new digital system says a prescribed fee is paid. Check the current fee on the portal.
- Bring or upload your original CNIC. Blood relatives can apply for you if they prove the relation.
- Some documents are not attested at all, e.g. domicile, photographs, invitation letters and visa notes.
