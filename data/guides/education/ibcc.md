# IBCC attestation and equivalence (Matric, FSc, O/A Levels)

The Inter Boards Coordination Commission (IBCC) attests school certificates (SSC/Matric,
HSSC/Intermediate) and issues equivalence for O/A Levels and other foreign school
qualifications. Bachelor's applicants usually need this.

## Attestation rules

- IBCC attests certificates, diplomas, mark sheets and result cards issued by recognised Boards (BISEs/BTEs).
- Your document must first be **verified by the Board that issued it**. The verification must be available online in the IBCC Attestation Portal.
- To attest your HSSC certificate, also submit a copy of your SSC (Matric) certificate.
- If your certificates were attested before 2010, you need fresh verification from your Board.
- Certificates found fake are kept and not returned.
- Only blood relatives may apply on your behalf.

## What to bring or send

- A printout of the application form from the IBCC Attestation Portal.
- The original certificate, diploma or marks sheet, plus one copy.
- The paid fee challan or a printout of the online fee receipt.
- A photocopy of your CNIC and your father's CNIC.

The fee amount is shown on the portal when you apply. It is not listed on IBCC's information pages.

## Three ways to apply

Register at **https://attest.ibcc.edu.pk/** first.

- **Appointment (morning service):** log in, book an appointment, pay via 1Bill or a challan at a
  UBL branch, then visit the IBCC office. Documents are attested the same or the next day.
- **Courier:** book a courier appointment, pay, then send the application form, payment receipt,
  original and photocopy of the document, and a CNIC copy. IBCC sends them back to you.
- **Express (evening service):** only at the Islamabad and Lahore offices. Visit with originals plus one photocopy.

## O Level and A Level equivalence

- **O Levels (GCSE/IGCSE/GCE O)** are treated as equivalent to SSC (Matric) if the scheme of studies is met:
  - Studied in Pakistan: 8 subjects, including compulsory English, Mathematics, Urdu, Islamiyat and Pakistan Studies, plus 3 electives, with at least grade E.
  - Studied abroad: 5 subjects, including English and Mathematics, plus 3 electives, with at least grade E.
- **A Levels** are treated as equivalent to HSSC (FSc/FA), subject to SSC-level equivalence first.
  Requirements depend on the stream (Pre-Medical, Pre-Engineering, General/Computer Science, Humanities).

### How to apply for equivalence
1. Register and log in at https://equivalence.ibcc.edu.pk
2. Submit the online application with your personal details, scanned certificates and CNIC copy.
3. Pay the equivalence fee (1Bill, or a challan at any UBL branch).
4. Track your application from the dashboard. If you apply by courier, send the form, payment receipt, certificates (originals and copies) and a CNIC copy to IBCC.

## Office hours

Monday to Thursday, 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM (as listed on ibcc.edu.pk).
