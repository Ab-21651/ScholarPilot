# Family Registration Certificate (NADRA)

## What it is

A Family Registration Certificate (FRC) is issued by NADRA under Rule 19 of the NADRA (NIC)
Rules, 2002. It is a record of your family composition and is "generally used for
immigration, visa processing and other legal or administrative purposes".

Students often need it for need-based scholarships that ask for proof of family members
(for example Italian regional DSU grants), and for passports of children under 10.

## Types

- **By birth:** parents and siblings.
- **By marriage:** spouse and children.
- **By adoption:** guardian and adopted family members.
- **By all:** both of the above.

## Who can get one

All family members should be registered with NADRA and have a valid 13-digit ID number.
Deceased family members who were never registered (and, in some cases, living ones) can
also be included, based on the information the applicant provides.

## What you need

- Your 13-digit ID number (CRC, Juvenile Card, CNIC, NICOP or POC).
- Apply at a NADRA Registration Centre.
- Fees and processing time are on NADRA's fee page (linked from the FRC page). Check the current fee there.
