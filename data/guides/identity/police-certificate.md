# Police character certificate

## Who issues it

The Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA) says a police character certificate must be issued
by the local **District Police Officer (DPO)**, or in tribal areas by the concerned
Political Agent.

## Attestation

MOFA lists police character certificates among the documents it attests. See the guide
"MOFA attestation and apostille" for how attestation works.

## Do I need one?

Some visa and residence-permit applications ask for one. Check your visa checklist (see the
visa guides) before getting it. Application steps and fees depend on your province, so check
your provincial police's official website or service centre.
