# Pakistani passport (DGI&P)

The Directorate General of Immigration & Passports (DGI&P) issues passports. Students
need an ordinary passport, which is issued to all citizens of Pakistan for travel abroad.

## Fees (ordinary e-passport)

As listed on the DGI&P "General Requirements for Passport" page:

| Pages | Validity | Normal | Urgent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 36 | 5 years | Rs 9,000 | Rs 15,000 |
| 36 | 10 years | Rs 13,500 | Rs 22,500 |
| 72 | 5 years | Rs 16,500 | Rs 27,000 |
| 72 | 10 years | Rs 24,750 | Rs 40,500 |

- A 100-page passport also exists, but its fee is not given in the page text. Check the fee list on dgip.gov.pk.
- **Fast track** service is available in 47 cities (DGI&P notice of 6 January 2025). Its fee is published only as an image on https://dgip.gov.pk/updates/passport-fast-track.php, so check it there.
- At passport offices, fees are paid through a QR code (for example EasyPaisa, JazzCash or online banking).

## Validity

- Passports are issued for 5 or 10 years.
- Children under 15 get a 5-year passport only.
- Visa offices check passport validity. If your passport will expire soon, renew it before you apply for a visa.

## Processing time

- Urgent: 5 working days. Normal: 21 working days (from DGI&P's "Passport Application Priority" section).
- Another section of the same page mentions shorter times (4 and 10 working days). Plan with the longer times.
- Courier delivery times are not included for remote areas.

## Documents you need

### Adults (18 and over)
- Original valid CNIC (or NICOP) and a photocopy.
- Previous passport and a photocopy, if you had one.
- Government, semi-government or autonomous employees: a NOC from the department.
- Dual nationals: foreign passport and a photocopy.
- Lost passport: a police report mentioning the previous passport number.

### Minors (under 18)
- Aged 10 and above: an original, updated Child Registration Certificate (CRC) with fingerprints and photograph, or a Juvenile Card, with one photocopy.
- Below 10: an original CRC (Form "B") or a Family Registration Certificate (FRC), with one photocopy.
- Original valid CNICs or passports of both parents, with photocopies.
- The minor must come with a parent or court-appointed guardian. Divorced or separated parents need custody papers, or the other parent's presence or attested consent. If a parent has died, bring the death certificate.

## How to apply in person

At a Regional Passport Office (RPO) the steps are:
1. Customer service counter
2. Photo and token with QR code
3. Biometrics
4. Data entry
5. Verification
6. Interview and decision by the Assistant Director
7. Fee payment via QR code
8. Delivery

Bring your original CNIC (or CRC / B-Form if under 18).

## Online renewal

- Renewal is available online for Pakistanis in Pakistan and overseas: https://onlinemrp.dgip.gov.pk/renew-passport/
- You can renew if your passport has expired or has less than 1 year of validity left.
- Online fees are paid by credit or debit card.
- A new (first) passport for someone living in Pakistan is applied for in person at an RPO.
