# Japan student visa

## Where to apply

- Since **1 April 2024**, visa applications are made through **VFS Global / Gerry's Visa**, not at the
  Embassy (except diplomatic and official visas). https://visa.vfsglobal.com/pak/en/jpn/
- VFS contact: +92-51-8439346, INFO.JAPANPAK@VFSGLOBAL.COM
- Residents of **Sindh and Balochistan** apply for Japanese visas through the Consulate General of Japan in Karachi.
- For documents, booking and processing questions, contact VFS / Gerry's directly. Asking the Embassy to
  accept your application directly will not be accepted and may delay you.

## Processing time

- At the earliest, **6 working days** from applying at VFS / Gerry's, if there are no problems.
- Incomplete documents, or cases needing close examination, can take **several months**, so apply early.
- No visa is issued on the same day.

## If a visa is refused

The reason is not given. You can apply again **6 months** after the refusal.

## MEXT scholars

MEXT scholarship holders follow the instructions from the Embassy's education section and their host
university. The Embassy's MEXT announcements are at https://www.pk.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/education.html
