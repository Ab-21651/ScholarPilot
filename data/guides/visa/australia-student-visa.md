# Australia Student visa (subclass 500)

Home Affairs page last updated 5 November 2026.

## Key facts

- Stay: up to **6 years**, in line with your enrolment.
- Cost: **from AUD 2,500** (concessions apply in limited cases). Use Home Affairs' Visa Pricing Estimator for your exact cost.
- Work: up to **48 hours a fortnight** while your course is in session.
- Processing times: use Home Affairs' processing time guide tool. Applications lodged on or after
  14 November 2025 are processed under Ministerial Direction 115.

## You must

- apply **online** (ImmiAccount), usually from outside Australia
- be enrolled in a course and hold a valid **Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE)** when the visa is decided
- hold **Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC)** unless exempt
- be a **genuine student**: show that studying in Australia is the main reason for your visa
- meet the **character** and **health** requirements, and sign the Australian Values Statement if 18 or over
- meet the English requirement (see "English test rules for visas"; at-home online tests are not accepted)
- show financial capacity (see "Australia: financial capacity for a Student visa")
- not bring family members unless exempt

## Beware of paid helpers

Before you pay anyone to help with your visa, read Home Affairs' page "Who can help with your visa application".
