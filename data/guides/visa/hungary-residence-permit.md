# Hungary residence permit for study

## One application covers the visa

You apply for a **residence permit for study**. That application also covers the visa that lets you enter
Hungary to collect the permit, so there is **no separate visa application**. You enter on a **type D visa**
and collect the residence permit card in Hungary.

## Where to apply

At the Hungarian mission (embassy or consulate) in the country of your nationality or where you live. For
Pakistani students, that means the Embassy of Hungary in Islamabad. Its website did not show application
details when we checked, so contact the Embassy to book an appointment.

## Documents

- Proof of purpose: admission letter or student status certificate
- Proof of language skills adequate for your studies
- Proof that tuition is paid (Stipendium Hungaricum covers tuition)
- Proof of subsistence and health insurance (see "Hungary: proof of means and insurance for a study permit")
- A real Hungarian address written on the form (no separate document needed)
- Valid passport, and a ticket or money for the return journey
- One passport photo. Your photo and fingerprints are taken when you apply.

## Fee, time and validity

- Fee when applying abroad: **€110** (OIF fee list, last edited 30 January 2025).
- Administrative processing time is **15 days**, and a decision on a study permit is made **within 60 days** of applying.
- The type D visa allows one entry and 30 days' stay, and is valid for 3 months. If you do not enter within 3 months, the permit becomes invalid.
- The permit is valid for **at least 1 year and at most 3 years** (shorter if your course is shorter).
- You can work up to **30 hours a week** during term.

## Stipendium Hungaricum scholars

- After you accept the scholarship you receive a **Letter of Acceptance** (from your university) and a
  **Letter of Award** (from Tempus Public Foundation). You need both for the visa.
- Book an appointment at the Hungarian consulate **as soon as possible**; it can take up to **30 days** to issue a visa.
- All awardees need a **medical certificate** of satisfactory health (template on stipendiumhungaricum.hu).
- The scholarship does not provide visas or benefits for family members.
