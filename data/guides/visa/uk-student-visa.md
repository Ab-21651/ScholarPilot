# UK Student visa

## Who can apply

You can apply if you are 16 or over and you:
- have been offered a place on a course by a licensed student sponsor (your university)
- have enough money for your course and living costs (see "UK: money you need for a Student visa")
- can speak, read, write and understand English (see "English test rules for visas")
- have parental consent if you are 16 or 17

## When to apply and how long it takes

- From outside the UK, the earliest you can apply is **6 months before your course starts**.
- A decision usually takes **3 weeks** from outside the UK. You may be offered a faster decision for an extra fee.

## Costs

- Visa application fee: **£558** from outside the UK, for each person.
- **Immigration Health Surcharge (IHS):** **£776 per year** for students. For example, £1,552 for a 2-year visa.
  - Visa of 6 months or less, applied from outside the UK: no surcharge.
  - More than 6 months but less than a year: you pay a full year.
  - Over a year: the yearly cost plus half the yearly cost if it is 18 months or less; 2 full years if it is more than 18 months but less than 2 years.

## Documents

- A current passport.
- Your **CAS** (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies) from your university.
- You may also need: proof of money, an ATAS certificate (some subjects), **tuberculosis test results**,
  consent from a sponsor if you were sponsored in the last 12 months, and parental consent if under 18.

## TB test (required for Pakistan)

- Pakistan is on the UK's list. If you are coming for 6 months or more and have lived in Pakistan
  recently, you need a TB test.
- You must use a **Home Office approved clinic**. The certificate is valid for **6 months from the date of your x-ray**.
- Approved clinics in Pakistan (gov.uk list, updated 20 February 2026; fees are for age 11 and above):
  - **Islamabad:** AMC, G-6 Markaz (Rs 15,000); IOM Migration Health Assessment Centre: Maroof
    International Hospital F-10, or IDC Pakland Square G-8 (Rs 14,000)
  - **Lahore:** AMC, 8-B Chaudhary Ali Anwar Road (Rs 15,000); IOM, House 70, H-Block, Gulberg III (Rs 14,000)
  - **Karachi:** IOM, Plot F-14, PECHS Block 6, Shahra-e-Faisal (fee not listed)
  - **Mirpur (AJK):** IOM, House 66-A, Sector F-2 (Rs 14,000)
- Check the gov.uk clinic list before booking, because clinics and fees can change.
