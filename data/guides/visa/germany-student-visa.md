# Germany student visa

Stays in Germany longer than 90 days need a **national (type D) visa**.

## Where to apply

- **Embassy in Islamabad:** Islamabad Capital Territory, Punjab, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu & Kashmir.
- **Consulate General in Karachi:** Sindh and Balochistan.

## How to apply

1. Register on the **Consular Services Portal**: https://digital.diplo.de/studium
2. Answer the questionnaire; the portal then shows the documents you need.
3. Upload clear scans (A4).
4. A visa officer checks them and either sends you an appointment or asks for more documents.

## Documents you must upload from the start

- Previous degree certificates (school, bachelor's or past master's degrees)
- Degree transcripts
- Proof of tuition fee payment, if your course charges fees

If these are missing, your online registration stays on hold. The full checklist is shown in the portal.

## Proof of finances

You usually need a blocked account of **€992 per month (€11,904 per year)**, or another accepted proof.
See the guide "Germany: blocked account and proof of finances".

## Fee and processing time

- Visa fee: **€75** for a long-term (type D) visa, paid **in cash in PKR** at the current exchange rate at the counter.
- Processing times vary a lot depending on the purpose of stay. Incomplete applications take longer, so apply early.
