# Italy study visa and residence permit

## Visa type

University study needs a **long-stay national visa (type D)**, which can last from **91 to 365 days**.
Italy has separate study visas for enrolling in a university degree, for post-graduate study, and for
exchange programmes.

## Document checklists (Embassy of Italy, Islamabad)

The Embassy publishes a checklist PDF for each study visa (updated June 2026). Download the one for your case:
- Enrolling in a university: https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MI10_VISTO-DI-STUDIO_ITA_Islamabad_finale.pdf
- Post-graduate study: https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MI11_Study-Visa-POST-LAUREA_Islamabad_ITA.pdf
- University exchange: https://ambislamabad.esteri.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/MI12_VISTO-PER-SCAMBIO-UNIVERSITARIO_Islamamabad_ITA.rev_.pdf

The Embassy notes that submitting the required documents does not guarantee a visa.

## Money

The required means of subsistence are set in an official table, linked from vistoperitalia.esteri.it
(published by the Polizia di Stato). Check the current amount there.

## Health insurance

For study, you need a private health insurance policy covering illness, accidents and maternity, or you
can enrol voluntarily in Italy's national health service (SSN).

## After you arrive: residence permit

- Within **8 working days** of entering Italy, apply for a **residence permit (permesso di soggiorno)**.
- For study, it is requested through a postal kit (at post offices with a "Sportello Amico" desk), followed
  by an appointment at the Questura (police headquarters) for fingerprints.
