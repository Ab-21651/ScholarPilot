# Italy: family income documents for DSU grants

Italian regional "right to study" (DSU) grants are need-based, so you must prove your family's income
and assets. Each region runs its own agency (e.g. ER.GO in Emilia-Romagna, EDISU in Piedmont) and its
own call, so always read your region's call ("bando"). The rules below are from ER.GO and EDISU.

## What you must submit (ER.GO)

- If your family has income or assets abroad, submit documents proving your family's economic condition.
- They must be **issued by the competent authorities** in your country, **in the year you apply**.
- They must show the family's **income for the previous year** and **assets as of 31 December of the previous year**.
- You must submit **new documents every year**.
- Upload them only through ER.GO's Dossier Utente. You never need an agency or intermediary.

## Legalisation or apostille

- Each document must be **legalised**, or carry an **apostille**. An apostille replaces diplomatic or
  consular legalisation (see the guide "MOFA attestation and apostille").
- Pakistan is not on ER.GO's list of countries exempt from legalisation.

## Translation into Italian

All documents must be translated into Italian, either:
- by an official translator or accredited interpreter in your home country, or
- by a sworn translator in Italy.

## EDISU (Piedmont)

Certificates must be issued by the competent foreign authorities, translated by an official translator,
and legalised. Students from outside the EU send the full set of consular documents by registered post
(with return receipt) or by courier with proof of delivery.

## Useful Pakistani documents

A NADRA **Family Registration Certificate** is the official record of family composition (see the FRC
guide). Which income and asset documents are accepted is set in each region's call, so check it there.
