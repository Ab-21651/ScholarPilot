# UK: money you need for a Student visa

## Course fees

You need enough money to pay your course fees for one academic year (up to 9 months). The amount
is shown on your **CAS** (Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies). Fees you have already paid count.

## Living costs

- **£1,529 per month** (for up to 9 months) for courses in London
- **£1,171 per month** (for up to 9 months) for courses outside London

"London" means the City of London and the 32 London boroughs. You need extra money for each family member who comes with you.

## The 28-day rule

- The money must be in your account for at least **28 days in a row**.
- The end of that 28-day period must be within **31 days** of the date you apply.
- gov.uk's example: if you apply on 1 January, you must show the money was in your account for at
  least the 28-day period ending on 1 December.

## Do Pakistani applicants need to show bank statements?

Pakistan is **not** on the UK's list of countries whose students can skip financial evidence, so plan
to show proof of funds. If a scholarship or official sponsor pays your costs, read gov.uk's financial
evidence guidance to see how sponsorship counts:
https://www.gov.uk/guidance/financial-evidence-for-student-and-child-student-route-applicants
