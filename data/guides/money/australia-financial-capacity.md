# Australia: financial capacity for a Student visa

Home Affairs page last updated 5 November 2026.

## What you must cover

At a minimum, enough money for:
- your travel
- **12 months of course fees** (or a pro-rata amount if your course is shorter)
- **12 months of living costs** for you and any family members coming with you
- school fees for school-age children who come with you

## Living cost amounts (per 12 months)

- Student: **AUD 29,710**
- Partner coming with you: AUD 10,394
- Child coming with you: AUD 4,449

For a shorter stay: divide the yearly amount by 365 and multiply by the number of days.

## Alternative: parents' or partner's income

Instead, you can show that your parents or partner earned at least **AUD 87,856** in the 12 months
before you apply (**AUD 102,500** if family members come with you). Both parents' incomes can be
combined. Evidence must be official government documents, such as tax assessments less than 12
months old. Bank statements or letters from employers are not accepted for this option.

## Evidence

Evidence can include deposits with a financial institution, government loans or loans from a
financial institution, and scholarships. If the document checklist asks for financial evidence,
attach it when you apply, because the application can be refused without further requests. Home
Affairs may also ask for it later.
