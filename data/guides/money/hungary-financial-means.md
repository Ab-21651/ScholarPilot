# Hungary: proof of means and insurance for a study permit

## Proof of subsistence

Hungary's immigration authority (OIF) accepts any one of:
- a bank certificate in your name
- a sponsor's notarised declaration, with proof that the sponsor can pay
- an income certificate from an employer or tax authority
- other credible proof, **such as a scholarship certificate**

OIF's page does not state a minimum amount. Stipendium Hungaricum holders use their scholarship documents.

## Tuition

Attach proof that your tuition fee has been paid (Stipendium Hungaricum covers tuition).

## Health insurance

You must prove coverage for the full range of health services: through social insurance, through an
international agreement, or by showing enough money to pay for healthcare. OIF does not state an amount.
