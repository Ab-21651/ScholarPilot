# Germany: blocked account and proof of finances

For a German student visa you must show you can pay for your stay. Most students use a
**blocked account (Sperrkonto)**.

## How much

According to the German Missions in Pakistan, for all visa applications submitted from
**1 January 2025** the required amount is **€992 per month, or €11,904 per year**.
The Embassy or Consulate can ask for up to two years in particular cases. DAAD's
study-in-germany site gives the same €11,904 yearly figure.

## Opening the account

- The account is usually opened for one year, unless your stay is shorter.
- Your signature on the account-opening form must be attested by the Embassy in Islamabad,
  the Consulate General in Karachi, or the Honorary Consul in Lahore.
- That attestation costs **€25 (about Rs 5,400, depending on the exchange rate)**. It is separate from the visa fee.

## Other ways to prove finances

- **Formal obligation letter (Verpflichtungserklärung)** from a sponsor in Germany, with the sponsor's
  payslips and bank statements covering at least 6 months.
- **Scholarship:** an official scholarship can be accepted. The Mission says a scholarship awarded by a
  Pakistani university is not enough; among Pakistani scholarships, only an **HEC** scholarship is accepted.
